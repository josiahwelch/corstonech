S.W.A.T. Team

S.W.A.T. Team is our young adult Bible study, lead by our youth minister, Chris Stolle. Chris is an engaging and informative teacher and he holds S.W.A.T. Team meetings at 7:00 PM at Cornerstone Christian Church and on Facebook Live!

Sunday Morning Worship

We hold worship at 10:30 AM every Sunday. Service begins with our worship team leading the congregation through hymns and Contemporary Christian music. Minister Steve Stolle preaches a passionate and constructive sermon over various subjects in the Bible. As it is central to our Church, he holds communion in the middle of our service and it is open for all members and non-members alike.

C.I.A.

C.I.A. is our youth Bible study for our middle and high school students, lead by our youth minister, Chris Stolle. Chris hosts a fun activity for his students at the beginning of service then leads them through an engaging sermon afterwards!. He holds C.I.A. meetings at 7:00 PM at Cornerstone Christian Church and on Facebook Live!

And more!

Most Sundays during intermission, we will hold Children's Church for our younger members and non-members of our Church! Every Tuesday at 1:30 PM, Pastor Steve will post an online Bible Study on Facebook Live for all members and non-members to enjoy and to use!
